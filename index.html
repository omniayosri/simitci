How a simit is made

01

Twist

Two ropes of dough, twisted by hand into a ring.

02

Pekmez dip

A quick bath in grape molasses for the deep color and shine.

03

Sesame

Rolled through a mountain of sesame. No bald spots allowed.

04

Bake

Into the stone oven until crisp, golden and loud when you bite.

Feed the martı

Every Istanbul ferry has its regulars. Tap the simit to break off a bite, then throw it to the seagulls. Feed them 10 of the 14 bites and the next simit is on us.
